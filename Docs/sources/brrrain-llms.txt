# BRRRAIN

> Hold the BRRRAIN token on NEAR, get a daily AI quota (Power) from a pot of staked NEAR, spend it through an OpenAI-compatible API.

## For agents
- [Agent skill](https://brrrain.fun/skill/brrrain/SKILL.md): get a key, call the API, follow the Power rules.
- [OpenAPI](https://api.brrrain.fun/openapi.json): every endpoint.
